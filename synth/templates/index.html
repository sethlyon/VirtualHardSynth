Control surface guide

Real, live controls

Keys, bank/program arrows, the encoder, patch pads, volume (knob A), pitch/mod wheels, HOLD (sustain), LATCH, octave/transpose, and PANIC all really talk to the running FluidSynth instance and patchbridge.py — anything you do here shows up on the physical CYD panel too, since both drive the exact same backend state. The Metronome and Quantize controls in the top bar drive the same looper that lives on the CYD's loop screen. The RECORD/PLAY/STOP transport on the surface below is a separate, local audition recorder for quickly hearing back a phrase you just played - it doesn't touch the looper or persist anywhere.

Intentionally disabled

Knobs B/C/D (reverb/chorus/filter), ARP, CHORD, and SCALE are dimmed because the current engine doesn't expose those parameters or behaviors yet — hover any dimmed control for why. Nothing here fakes sound it isn't actually making.

Loop quantization

Snaps recorded and overdubbed notes to a rhythmic grid at the metronome's tempo, and rounds the base loop's length up to the nearest bar so it cycles cleanly on the beat. Applies going forward only — anything already recorded stays as played.
